## Characters
- **Twinsen (#47 harbour, #38, #23)**: tunic is **ankle-length**, A-line, wide, light blue, with an
  **orange/red belt** at the waist; wide sleeves to the wrist; big gold medallion on the chest; round
  head, high hairline, dark-brown short hair and a small ponytail tuft at the back; shoes only below the hem.
  Reads masculine through the broad shoulders, thick neck, round full face, short hair — not through the hem.
- **Baldino (#32)**: an old **Grobo** (grey elephant-folk, trunk, big ears), robe, inventor's workshop.
- Townsfolk: Quetches in simple tunics, Rabbibunnies, Grobos, Sups; strong, simple silhouettes, saturated
  clothing colours (yellow, red, green, blue).

## Citadel Island (#02, #13, #37, #38, #47, #52, #53)
- **Terraced island**: levels separated by steep grey-violet rock cliffs (strong rock texture), not smooth hills.
- Paths and quays paved in grey rectangular setts; quay edges = rough rubble-stone embankment walls down to
  the sea; square white stone bollards with teal/green rails between them.
- Ground between paths: brown soil, lawns with **tulip beds** (red/orange/yellow), round two-tier trees.
- Houses: rubble-stone walls (irregular stone texture) with brown wooden-plank/shingle roofs; or grey stone
  boxes with flat/low roofs, teal trim and light-blue lattice balustrades on the roof edge.
- Street lamps: post with two curled arms holding a globe (blue or white).
- Red slatted benches. Flower pots. Wooden signposts.
- **Lighthouse (#49, #50)**: square tapering stone tower on a rock outcrop, small balcony, lantern room on top.
- **Ferry (#53)**: white-and-red steamer; stone pier with bollards.
- Sea: saturated cyan-blue with painted wave texture; sky: soft blue with streaky painted clouds.

## Interiors (#20, #25, #29, #32, #51, #54–56, #59)
- Iso view with only the far walls; walls are **thick** with a rounded/bevelled top edge showing the wall
  section; stone-block or panelled walls; floors in tiles (terracotta, checker), patterned carpets.
- Dense, characterful furniture: bookcases with books, desks with lamps and papers, telescopes, machines with
  dials, chests, jars, pipes, railings, ladders, two-level rooms with balconies.
- Warm lighting, lamps on walls.

## Industrial / project look (#07, #08 "elevator to the Undergas", #11, #60–61 space port)
- Metal deck plates with rivets, **yellow-black hazard stripes**, red steel frames, teal panels, crates,
  barrels, big machines with fans/dials, lavender-grey metal decks.

## Zeelich (#17, #18, #22, #23 Otringal; #19 palace)
- Overcast pale-grey sky, misty; lavender-grey paving with lighter borders; **teal metal railings**;
  stylised buildings with blue-tile walls; teal horned ring arch; striped awnings; stone stairs.

## Underground (#03 sewers, #10, #34–36 caves, #62 temple)
- Dark slate stone blocks and cliffs; clusters of **stalagmite spikes**; clusters of round blue rocks;
  dark iron railings; lantern posts with small blue globe lamps; water channels; wooden bridges;
  mine rails and wheels.
