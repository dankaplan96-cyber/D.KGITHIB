## The spot
Citadel, the knoll at the promenade's south end, between the arched house and the water office (the blue roof),
x −72…−64, z 16…20. It is the leftover of the first shelf: FLAT-TOWN's inland wall stops at z 16.4, the south wall
starts at z 18.8, and the mound in between kept its natural faces (REVIEW-CITADEL-ART c cut its east face back). The
user's view is reproduced in Follow/Far with Twinsen at (−63.5, 17.5) facing west (`captures/p121/user-spot-follow-far.jpg`).

It was noticed before. C66 (order 66.2, GTX 1050) reported "a rock and a wedge by the walls". C66-FIX levelled the wedge
and wrote "the south-end 'rock' is the knoll's ground (left)". That was wrong: it is a pile of cliff rocks, not ground.

## Why it looked like this
- `cliffs.js buildCliffRocks` puts a faceted rock every 1.9 m on any slope steeper than 1.05, each one along its own
  fall line. On a long straight cliff the neighbours overlap by design, and their faces lie nearly in one plane. On
  this 6 m mound, seven rocks stood on three faces (east, north and south) and **crossed** one another. The faces of
  one ran through the next. One rock was 61 % inside another. The pale top corner of a rock behind stuck out of a
  darker one's face: that is **the floating shard** in the photo. Its "lilac" is the same texture on facets that face
  away from the sun.
- "Jammed into the wall": the corner rock stood 19 cm from the end of the inland wall. Nothing cut it at this spot, but
  it filled the corner. In two other places masonry really did cut rocks: the fort's south wall over the hostel yard
  (−38.4, −51.4), and the climb's yard wall (−33.2, −48.1), where a pale shard showed between the coping and the steps.
  `dropRocks` only checked the rocks' corners, ran before those walls existed (the climb is built in main.js after the
  world), and missed walls thinner than a rock.
- The same fault elsewhere in the game: the boulders merged into one mesh by the room builders (`underground.js`
  rockWallRun and the cave rims in c3shaft, c3deep, c3gallery and Tralu's cave), where one boulder sits half inside
  its neighbour. The sweep could not see any of these before: every rock in Citadel is one mesh, and the rooms' rocks
  are merged into the room's meshes.

## The fix (cause, game-wide)
- `cliffs.js settleRocks` (Citadel's cliff rocks, the only generator of faceted cliff rocks):
  - Every pair of rocks is measured as solids: vertical rays on a 0.2 m grid; each chunk is a closed solid with an
    exact volume.
  - A pair is **bad** when 45 % or more of the smaller rock (or of a smaller chunk) is inside the other, or when the
    fall lines differ by more than 50° and a sixth of the smaller is inside.
  - Each group joined by bad pairs becomes **one outcrop**: the convex hull of its corners, which has no slivers. This
    happens only where the hull adds little rock over the ground (≤ 15 % of the group's visible rock + 0.3 m³).
    Otherwise the most-buried rocks go, until no bad pair is left. A rock that a new outcrop holds half of also goes.
  - No random draw changes: the placements are still the 509 (rocks.mjs).
  - Result: 55 rocks became 27 outcrops, 53 rocks went, and 8 536 triangles remain (was 9 910).
- `world.js`: the masonry predicates (the climb, the promenade, the plateau stair) now run **before** settling, inside
  buildCliffRocks (`cut`), and also test points every 0.15 m along each rock's edges.
  - `rocksOutOfMasonry` runs once everything on Citadel is built (main.js, after the asylum's climb and the hatches).
    It removes a cliff rock with a corner or an edge point 10 cm or more inside a box collider.
- `rocksettle.js settleRockRanges` (new): every mesh that `kit.js Batch` or `props.js Kit` merges records the index
  ranges of rock geometries (`rockGeometry`, `rockGeo`: `userData.rock` → `rockRanges`).
  - When a builder finishes, a rock that is 45 % or more inside another rock of comparable size (up to 5× its volume)
    collapses to a point. The buffers keep their sizes and nothing else moves.
  - It ran in every region and room. It left out 41 boulders: c3shaft 11, c3deep 4, c3gallery 20, Tralu's cave 6. No
    other area had such a pair.
- Nothing was added: the fixes remove or merge rocks. Collision is unchanged, because the cliff rocks were visual only
  (the terrain collides) and the cave walls collide by their boxes.
- `?rocks=old` turns off settling and the masonry pass, for the user's side-by-side comparison.
- Cost (SwiftShader, a shared machine): settling takes 111–316 ms once, in Citadel's loading step. There is no
  per-frame cost. The room builders' pass costs a few ms per cave build; I did not measure it separately.

## The sweep (`tests/overlap-sweep.mjs`, `tests/lib/sweep.js`)
- **(f) rock in rock**: two rock parts (including parts of the same mesh) where 50–97 % of the smaller is inside the
  other, and the two are of comparable size.
- **(g) rock through masonry**: a rock through a built part by more than 5 cm on 3 or more rays. These do not count:
  a part with the rock's own look (a cave's rock wall), and a floor or ledge the rock is bedded in (the overlap is in
  the lowest 35 % of the rock).
- **(c)** now also counts a rock floating over the ground. Before this change every rock was exempt from (c); now only
  a sunk rock is.
- Final run: every area scanned (144). Categories a–e have 0 open, as before; Citadel's known (d) count went from 242
  to 216 because fewer rocks remain. The verdict is FAIL (1 visual), only for the 19 open (g) findings handed over
  below.
- Counts, open/known, all 144 areas. "Before" is the build before the fix, read with the new checks. Citadel is from
  the run before the fix; the other areas are from a run that already had the new tagging, before the room pass.

## Decisions for the user (visible changes; nothing ships without the user's pick, P107)
1. **The knoll by the water office**: its seven crossing rocks are now one outcrop plus the pale rock behind it.
   Recommended: keep it. Alternative: carry the promenade's inland wall on to the south wall, so the knoll becomes
   masonry (FLAT-TOWN's rule for the town's edges). That would be a bigger change to a place the user has kept.
2. **Citadel's other cliff faces**: 27 crossing groups became outcrops and 53 buried rocks went. The faces read
   cleaner, with fewer vertical slivers and gaps. Recommended: keep all of it. `?rocks=old` shows the old look.
3. **The caves**: 41 boulders that sat half inside a neighbour are gone. The change is small.
