# P123 — Twinsen's tunic reads as "a sleeve inside the tunic" (the user, 24.11, photo of v155/v156)

The user: "Also the tunic — it looks like a sleeve inside the tunic; it shouldn't be like that"
(`captures/p122/user-twinsen-hand-v155.jpg`: a wide blue sleeve, then a separate lighter blue band, then the hand).
Base: `origin/ccr-c1c2c4b9-dep6ed` at 73c52e93. Worker TUNIC-SLEEVE. Evidence: **Automated runtime only**
(SwiftShader, headless Chromium, the worktree's files answered by Playwright request routing). Nobody played it, and
there was no real GPU.

## Why it looked like that
Twinsen's tunic is LBA2's cape. Its side is his sleeve: its weights ride the upper arm and forearm, and the hand comes
out under it. Under the side's edge, `twinsen_poly.py` built a **second, narrower cone** on the forearm bone (`cuffL/R`,
r 3.0 → 4.2 cm H-units, from 0.455 down to 0.398 H). CUFF-HEM (7.11) gave it a rolled rim (`cuffHemL/R`). It stuck out
**2 cm H (4.2 cm in the game) below the tunic's edge** (0.408 → 0.388 H). It also flared **past** the side
(x 0.232 against the side's 0.212). Its outward-facing cone normals caught the light more than the tunic's hanging side,
so it read lighter. The result looked like an inner sleeve poking out of the outer one. That band is the one in the
photo (`arm-closeups-before-after.jpg`, top row).

## The fix (sleeve end only)
`tools/blender/twinsen_poly.py`, `TUNIC_SLEEVE` (default on):
- `cuffL/R` and `cuffHemL/R` are no longer built.
- Over each arm, the tunic's own edge comes down by the cuff's length (`SLEEVE_DROP` = 0.020 H). This covers the
  75–105° columns round each side and eases back to the old hem by 45°/135°. The front, back and belt line keep their
  hem. The side therefore ends where the cuff ended. The sleeve is the same width and length, and the same amount of
  hand shows as before. The hand comes straight out of the tunic's side.
- The tunic's dark inner lip (`capeLip`) is the cloth's thickness along the tunic's whole hem. It follows the lowered
  edge, so the sleeve's rim is the same as the rest of the hem.
- The cuff's dark lid round the wrist (`cuffInL/R`) is now a flat oval 1.6 cm up inside the side (12 triangles, was a
  36-triangle tube and cap). It is narrower across, so it stays inside the cloth. Seen from below, the wrist goes up
  into a dark sleeve instead of an empty shell.
- `TW_SLEEVE_OLD=1` builds v156 exactly (checked: the same bytes as the build before the change).
  `TW_SLEEVE_HEM=1` builds option B.

**Byte-identical elsewhere.** The shipped `assets/poly/twinsen.json` is the new build spliced onto v156. Every triangle
corner the change did not move keeps v156's bytes. Checked: 14 142 corners kept and 198 moved, all in `cape` (114) and
`capeLip` (84) on the lowered edge. The cuff parts were removed, and 24 lid triangles are new. The head, hair (lock and
tie), face, eyes, mouths, medallion, cord, belt, robe, shins, shoes and **hands (the v154 fist)** are byte for byte
v156's, and so are the bones.

**Skeleton / sockets / animation:** unchanged. The bone list, order and bind positions are the same. No weights changed
on any unchanged corner. The lowered edge has the same weights as the side's hem (LBA2's: the forearm at the edge, as
the cuff had). `PALM`, the ball's hand bone and every grip point are unchanged.

## Options (switch in the game, no rebuild)
| | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| **A (default)** | The tunic's side to its end, the hand out of it, the dark inner lip at the rim like the rest of the hem | default |
| B | A plus a rolled hem of the tunic's blue round the sleeve's edge (1 cm, fading out into the front and back hem) | `?sleeve=hem` |
| old | v156: the cuff band under the sleeve | `?sleeve=old` |

The settings key `charSleeve` (`old`/`hem`) does the same where a URL cannot be given. Each variant is a small
triangle delta against `twinsen.json`: `assets/poly/twinsen-sleeveold.json` (87 KB) and `twinsen-sleevehem.json`
(71 KB), written by the new `tools/blender/polydelta.py`, which checks that the delta rebuilds the variant exactly. The
game fetches a delta only when it is asked for (`characters.js` `sleeveVariant` / `applyPolyDelta`). Checked:
`?sleeve=old` renders pixel-identical to v156, and `?sleeve=hem` pixel-identical to the hem build.

*Recommendation: A.* B's hem catches the light as a thin lighter line along the edge (see `arm-closeups-hem-option.jpg`).
That is close to the band the user objected to, and the rest of the tunic's hem has no roll.

## Sweep (the same fault elsewhere)
Twinsen is the only model built as a cape side over a separate narrower cuff cone. The cast's `cuff*` parts
(`cast_poly.py`, `ballastra_poly.py`) are designed trim bands, in their own trim colour, on fitted sleeves or trousers,
not a second sleeve under a wider one. Zoé has no cuff. The statue (`statue.js`) uses Twinsen's model, so it gets the
fix. Nothing else to change.

## Tools
- `tools/blender/nobpy`: Twinsen can now be rebuilt without Blender (`mathutils.Vector` setters, a 4×4 `Matrix`,
  `mathutils.bvhtree.BVHTree` ray casts, an empty `bmesh`). The nobpy build of v156 matches the shipped file to the
  JSON's last digit, except the medallion's cord (laid by ray casts, ≤ 1.2 mm off).
- `tools/blender/handpass.py` skips a non-model JSON in `assets/poly` (the deltas). It still skips Twinsen.

## Limitations
- The lid inside the sleeve is flat. From straight below, at a close distance no camera in the game uses, the side's
  edge is a single sheet. Only B gives it a visible thickness.
- The notebook cut's hand holds turn the wrist inside the sleeve. In the frames taken, no hand pokes out, but not
  every frame of the film was looked at.
- No real GPU. The colours in the pictures are SwiftShader's.

## Decision for the user

   (`?sleeve=old`, or revert this commit). *Recommendation: (a).*
