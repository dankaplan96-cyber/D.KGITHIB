# P120 · ZOE-HAIR · Zoé's hair made like Twinsen's · cloud, 24.11.2026

**The user (24.11, a photo of the screen on v150 and LBA2's box art):** "Zoé's hair should be like Twinsen's." On the
photo her hair reads as a pale smooth cap with a pale lump of a ponytail and a thin green band: from that angle she
looks bald. The box art (promotional art, used as a reference for the look only; not in the repository) has golden-blonde
hair pulled up into a big ponytail that rises from the crown and sweeps back with a curl, a round green tie at its root,
and a hairline that is clearly hair.

Starting revision: `d92890a3` (branch `ccr-c1c2c4b9-dep6ed`). All results here: **Automated runtime only** (SwiftShader,
headless Chromium). The look itself is for the user to judge (P107); nothing ships until they pick.

## Why she looked bald
- The model's hair colour `#e3af53` is close to her skin `#d9a679` in value, and the game's character ramp (LbaMaterial:
  past N·L 0.6 it mixes toward ×1.5 and 30 % white) turns any surface facing the fixed light from above into pale cream.
  Her swept-up hair over the skull faces up, so from the play and talk cameras it rendered as a near-skin cream cap.
- The game's output pass keeps 72 % of the chroma (renderer.js `GRADE`), so a gold that looks rich in lookdev reads sandy in
  play.
- The ponytail was small (a tube 3–6 cm round, rising 15 cm), the same pale colour, and the green band thin.

## What was built (`src/game/characters.js`)
1. **Twinsen's lock generalised, his look byte-identical.** `hsSpine`, `splineLock`, `hairBand` and `swapHair` now take a
   lock's own parameters (control points, width profile, section depth, tones, sheen, section points, ring counts, strand
   ridges, the tie's size, colours and the scalp it sits on). Twinsen's are `HS_TWINSEN` / `HB_TWINSEN`, built from the
   same constants and expressions as before. Proof: a hash of every vertex attribute of his built model in lookdev
   (`PHASE=hash`): default look `be7decb8` before and after, `?look=hairsmooth` `d99a536e` before and after (1,120,032
   bytes each).
2. **Zoé's hair** (`zoeHair`, applied in `lookModel` when the models load, so every Zoé gets it: the yard, the opening film,
   every chapter's Zoé, the hero's own Zoé body, the bronze statue — they all build from `assets/poly/zoe.json`):
   - the old ponytail and its band found on the model (pieces carried by the tail bones, in the hair or trim colour) and
     taken out; nothing else of the model is touched (face, eyes, brows, lashes, earrings, necklace, outfit, hands, feet);
   - a new lock swept along a spline with Twinsen's code: rooted under the scalp at the back of the crown, gathered in the
     tie, rising and swelling to about 13 cm thick over the top, sweeping back and down behind her head and tapering to a
     point; six soft strand ridges that twist a little toward the tip, golden tones from a deeper root to a lighter tip, a
     painted sheen band; smooth normals levelled for the ramp as Twinsen's lock (no cream top);
   - its weights pass from the head (to just past the tie) to tail0, tail1, tail2 along it, like the old ponytail's, so the
     rig's follow-through still sways it (no skeleton, bone or animation change);
   - a chunky green tie (her trims' green `#3f7b47` with a light and a dark side; 9.5 mm proud, 28 mm long) found on the
     model where the lock leaves the hair over the skull, as Twinsen's brass cuff is found on his scalp;
   - the hair over the skull (the model's own surface cut along the hairline) repainted gold `#c68818`, combed toward the tie
     in 13 soft streaks, darker by the tie, its normals levelled so the top of the head is no longer washed to cream.
3. **Switch** `?zoehair=a|b|c` (default **a**), `?zoehair=old` the model's own hair (v150). The same text in the setting
   `zoeHair` (no menu entry), like Twinsen's `charLook`. `?look=old` still only concerns Twinsen.

| variant | what | clearance (tail-carried hair to her head/neck/collar/back, 7 poses) |
|---|---|---|
| old | v150's ponytail and cap | 4.4 cm |

tie). One material as before (LbaMaterial, vertex colours), no textures. Twinsen unchanged (see above).

## Game-wide sweep
- Every Zoé is built from the one model (`assets/poly/zoe.json` via `createCharacter('zoe')`): the yard and home NPC, the
  opening film's actor, the chapters' Zoé (story/*.js `npc('zoe')`), the hero's Zoé body (`swapBody('zoe')`), the statue
  (statue.js) — the hair is applied once at load to that model, so all of them change together. No other character uses
  her model or her colours (the DRESS recolour has no Zoé variant).
- Portraits/thumbnails: none of Zoé exist as files (the dialogue box shows a name, not a portrait); the behaviour menu's
  portraits are live renders of the hero's own model (createCharacter), so they follow too. The painted family portrait
  in the house (props.js `portrait`) is a simple canvas of two heads, neither drawn blonde; not changed (it does not
  claim to be her; listed for the user if they want it to match).
- The bake: her sculpted fallback is not touched (LOOKS.zoe unchanged); the game run reports 66 bake hits, 1 miss, none of
  hers — no rebake needed.

## Remaining limitations
- The hair is a runtime rebuild of the loaded model (as Twinsen's spline lock), not re-exported from Blender
  (`tools/blender/zoe_poly.py` still builds the old ponytail; `zoe.json` unchanged). If the user keeps a variant, folding it
  into the Blender build is optional.
- Strand ridges and streaks are painted vertex colour; no texture. At the Far camera they read as soft shading only.
- "Behind her in the Follow camera": in this yard the tree and the fence sit between the Follow camera and her when
  Twinsen walks round her, so for that picture she is turned to face away from the Follow camera for one frame. The house
  picture is a copy of her model set in the room by the script (she is not in the house at the game's start).
- b and c were taken in lookdev only (the four sides, the talk pose, the walk from behind); in the game only old and a.

## Animator handoff
No skeleton, bone name, bind transform or socket changed. The lock is weighted head → tail0 → tail1 → tail2 along its
length (head to q 0.04, tail0 at q 0.22, tail1 at 0.5, tail2 at 0.82), as the old ponytail. The lock is longer and
reaches further from the tail bones' pivots than the old one, so the rig's existing follow-through (rig.js "ponytail
follows motion with lag") moves its tip a little more; no change was made to that motion, and none is needed for the
clearance (≥ 5 cm in every variant through the tested poses).

## Decisions for the user
1. **Which hair for Zoé:** a (default, the box art's tall sweep), b (lower and calmer), c (a with the curled tip), or
   keep old. Recommendation: **a**, or **c** if the curl of the box art matters more than the flick.
2. Keep the colour as is (deeper gold than v150, so it survives the game's grade), or lighter.
