# P152: chapter 2's start at the well, a talk with an absent Gilbert (and P150's statue check)

The user, playing the closed pilot (27.11): "At the start of chapter 2, Twinsen by the well: characters vanish, and he
talks with Gilbert, but Gilbert is not there."

Evidence labels: automated runtime only (SwiftShader, headless Chromium), plus source reading. Nobody played it on a
real GPU.

## Cause

Chapter 2 has the key `project` (`src/game/story/chapter1.js`, class `Chapter1`). Chapter 1 (`well`, `prologue.js`)
places Gilbert and Mira at the well. Chapter 1 ends in the cave. `Story.nextChapter` then calls `refreshCast` under the
chapter card with `noWalk`, and `Chapter1.place()` put both of them in the water office interior: Gilbert because
`!c1_water`, and Mira always. The office interior stands about 1240 m away. `begin()` then played the morning chain
`hero / gilbert c1_gil_morning / hero`. `ChapterBase.speaker('gilbert')` returned the office Gilbert (present and drawn,
but in another scene), and `Game.say` attached the line to him. So the two people at the well disappeared under the
card, and Gilbert's "Twinsen! Hours down there…" came from nobody on screen. The test reproduced it on the old build:
Gilbert's line had speaker `scene: false, d: 1240.3`, and Mira was gone from the well.

A second fault hid behind the first. `begin()` never recast after the morning lines. The first `refreshCast()` that came
later (OnePlace's recast when the room changes, on the first frame of play) put anyone already walking straight onto
their new place, because any non-walk recast cancelled a walk in progress.

The sweep found the same fault in more places (the chapter1 suite's `[talk]` log):
- Vesk's arrival on the pier: Gilbert answers his speech (`c1_gil_speech`) while he stands in the water office.
- Zoé's playable segments (chapter 2's office, chapter 3's lighthouse): her lines named her hidden NPC as the speaker
  instead of the body the player controls, so the bubble and mouth were on an invisible actor.

## Fix

- `chapter1.js place()`: while the morning lines play (`this.morning`, not saved), Gilbert and Mira stay on chapter 1's
  well marks. When the lines end, `refreshCast({ walk: true })` runs. Gilbert walks down to the quay to meet the
  project's ferry, as his own line says ("the chief engineer lands today; let him explain the bill to me"). He answers
  Vesk's speech from there, and after Vesk gives the task he walks to the water office. Mira walks to the office. Both
  office placements carry `via:` (the office's front door), so the walk ends at the door instead of a cut.
- `chapter1.js applyFlags()`: a save made under chapter 2's card, before the morning lines ended (`nextChapter` saves
  first), loaded with no objective and the chapter never started (`update` waits for `c1_started`). It now continues
  from the end of the morning.
- `story/index.js refreshCast`: a recast that gives a walking person the same place he is walking to lets the walk
  continue. A load or a chapter card (`noWalk`) still puts everyone straight on their places. This applies to every
  chapter and side story that calls `refreshCast()`.
- `story/base.js speaker()`: when the player controls a character's body (`hero.body`, for example Zoé), that
  character's lines belong to the hero.
- Game-wide guard, `main.js say()` and `speakerHere()`: no line is attached to a body that is not there. That covers a
  hidden NPC, or one in another scene and out of the camera's view; a cut-away that shows the speaker counts as there.
  Such a line plays as an off-screen voice: the name and words stay, with no bubble, mouth, face or two-shot on a body
  elsewhere. The line is recorded in `game.absentTalks` and logged as `[talk] absent speaker …`, and `tests/lib/boot.mjs`
  prints that log in every suite (as `TALK …`) so the cause can be fixed in the story. The guard never pulls the speaker
  to Twinsen, because radio lines name real people far away. For enemies only "drawn" counts: a guard the watch called
  off has `present = false` but still stands and speaks.

## P150: the square's statue

`tests/statue-150.mjs` (`SERVE=1`) writes two play-camera pictures (Twinsen 7 m in front of the statue, then 7 m to its
side) and three close views (front, left side, three-quarter right) to `captures/statue-150/`. The contact sheet is
`captures/statue-150/sheet.png` (1260 px wide). The pictures are not black.

What the first pictures showed (the P150 pose as committed): there is no raised arm and no clipping. The right arm was
bent forward, though, so the ball sat in front of his hip; from his right side it stood out in front of him at belt
height, which risks the same reading the user objected to. Changed in `statue.js`: the arm now hangs almost straight and
slightly out (`armR.sh.x -0.06, el.x -0.18, sh.z -0.3`), and the ball is smaller (scale 0.62, was 0.8) and sits beside
the thigh. In the new pictures the ball hangs at his right side, outside the robe. The upper arm is hidden under the
cape, as on the live Twinsen model, and nothing clips. This is a visible world change for the user to pick. The pictures
were inspected by me (automated, SwiftShader), not by a person.
