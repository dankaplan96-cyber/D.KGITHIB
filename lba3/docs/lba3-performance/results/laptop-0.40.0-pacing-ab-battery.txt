LBA3 pacing A/B — why the desktop app paced at ~56 Hz on battery (build271 full benchmark: "nothing drawn 56.5 Hz") while Chrome paced at 60
desktop app 0.40.0 (build 271, installed) vs Chrome 156 on the SAME files (extracted from the installed app.asar, served on localhost)
2026-10-30 11:03–11:38 · the user's Vega 8 laptop, ON BATTERY the whole time (88 → 69 %) · 1366×768 · runner: docs/lba3-performance/local/ (electron-bench.mjs, chrome-bench.mjs, pacing.mjs)
Each case: one game.bench.runAuto() (Auto settled Medium-eco in every case), with a "nothing drawn" probe of 120 refreshes before and after it
(the game's own benchIdle hook, as bench.run's probe). App runs use a separate bench profile (never the player's saves), launched by Playwright _electron.

SUMMARY
- The ~56 Hz did NOT reproduce: "nothing drawn" was 59.9 Hz (median 16.7 ms) in every case, before and after the run; Electron's screen reports 60 Hz.
  So on this laptop today the app's pacing on battery matches Chrome's. What differed in the build271 run (the user's manual start, the player's own profile
  and settings, the full benchmark's per-route probe, battery level) is still open.
- --disable-direct-composition in fullscreen is WORSE: street locks to 30 fps (frame p50 33.3, 33.3 fps vs 39.7), interior 58.3 with 33 ms frames. Not a fix.
  It does make the idle refresh steadier (1–2 of 120 intervals over 17.2 ms vs 11–23 with DirectComposition), windowed or fullscreen.
- Fullscreen vs windowed/maximized: no pacing difference (59.9 both); street fps 39.6–39.7 fullscreen, 37.8 maximized, 39.0 in a 1280×720 window.
- Chrome on the same 0.40.0 files: street 36.8 (vs app 39.6–39.7), interior 60 in both. Street stays ~37–40 fps on battery in every case (GPU p50 17.4–19.1 ms).
