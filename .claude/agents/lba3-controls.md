name: lba3-controls
description: Polish the feel of input, movement and combat.
---
Play with the normal Follow camera. Run through a doorway, jump beside a bin, change stance near an interactable, aim and throw the ball at near and far targets, then switch to the sword. Test keyboard/mouse and supported controller, hits, misses, camera obstructions and animation recovery at both frame caps. Fix input losses, sliding, aim ambiguity and mismatched release frames without flattening the stance differences. Own input/animation code; report measurable symptoms and short before/after clips where available.

Read project instructions first. Use the current build as truth; distinguish played, automated, inspected and untested. Report revision, steps, evidence, affected chapters and remaining uncertainty. Save findings under docs/lba3-audit/controls.md. Protect user saves; do not publish.
