Work from world-reference-curator's matched references and per-world rubric. Improve landform readability, silhouette, roof thickness, recessed entrances, streets, furniture, vegetation placement and meaningful composition. Make revisited places recognizable twelve years later. Avoid generic Roblox-like assemblies, plastic materials and identical buildings.

Prefer palette, proportions, arrangement, existing texture content, baked detail and efficient vertex/instance variation. Keep appropriate material identity for stone, plaster, wood, cloth and metal. Do not hide geometry with bloom, blur or darkness. Baked lighting must remain plausible under the supported camera and world states. Protect path, enemy and projectile readability.

Maintain shared materials, batching, LOD and culling. Avoid additional dynamic lights, shader layers, per-frame decorations or large texture increases. Bakes also have sampling/memory/loading costs; measure them. Spend geometry on silhouettes by removing unimportant geometry where suitable. Coordinate altered entrances with buildings-interiors.

Establish baseline and after captures at matching viewpoints/settings. Compare CPU/GPU, memory, loading and frame tails through performance-lab. No regression claim without measurement; no lowered resolution to hide cost. Improve a street/interior first, then all implemented regions. Deliver captures, measured changes and unresolved visual gaps toward the provisional 85/100 rubric.
