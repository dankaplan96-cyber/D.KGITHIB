name: lba-chapter-balance
description: "Balances active playtime, content depth and difficulty across every chapter without padding."
---


The user explicitly wants ALL chapters broadly comparable in length, with no extremely short or trivially easy chapters. Do not focus only on Chapter 2. Establish the actual current chapter count and main-route structure before setting targets. Do not silently renumber chapters to hide missing content.

Measure active gameplay per chapter: exploration, puzzle solving, combat, interaction and necessary travel. Report dialogue/cinematics, loading, retries, idle time and optional content separately. Record actual scripted replay time separately from estimated blind first-play time; an agent familiar with solutions cannot stand in for a new player. Use human first-play evidence when available and label estimates honestly.

Set a provisional common target T from the campaign's planned main-route duration divided by its actual chapter count, informed by measured content. As an initial review band use 0.75T–1.25T; this is a design flag, not a hard timer. Chapters outside the band require meaningful edits or explicit justified exceptions. Opening and epilogue may be shorter only if treated honestly as prologue/aftermath, not used to excuse short core chapters. The prior ~15-hour goal includes optional play; don't force 15 hours of mandatory content by multiplying artificial chapter durations. Reconcile the duration plan with the current synopsis.

Measure difficulty through player decisions and skill: clue clarity, puzzle steps, new applications of learned mechanics, enemy coordination, resource pressure, available recovery and retry cost. Difficulty should build fairly rather than be identical. A late chapter must not collapse to a few easy conversations. Avoid opaque clues, huge health pools, sudden damage spikes and repeated enemy waves as substitutes for depth.

For every chapter create a table: current measured/estimated time, target band, main activities, challenge, payoff, underdeveloped segment, planned fix and retest. Improve thin chapters with causally connected investigations, puzzles, alternate routes and consequential encounters using existing spaces. Shorten tedious long chapters through shortcuts and removing repetition. Preserve optional content as optional. Test early item discovery and different quest order.

Acceptance: every core chapter has a substantial playable arc, no unresolved extreme length outlier or trivial progression, a recorded duration basis, and a coherent transition. Never enforce parity through mandatory waiting or slowing movement. Coordinate quest edits with campaign-director and world-campaign-finisher.
